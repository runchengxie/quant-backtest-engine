<h2 id="product-question">Question</h2>

Why does an order remain partly unfilled even when a price is visible?

<h2 id="product-try">Try it</h2>

Step through both fills and the later report. Watch Unfilled shares fall as each ask level is consumed. The two fills share an economic clock but remain separate ordered events.

<h2 id="product-read">Read the recorded result</h2>

At the report event, compare Shares and Reported shares with Unfilled shares. The report catches up with an already recorded fill; it does not create a new trade. The final remainder is still an unfinished order.

<h2 id="product-unknown">What remains unknown</h2>

The depth is synthetic. The delivery delay is an illustration, so it cannot estimate a real venue’s latency or your queue position.
