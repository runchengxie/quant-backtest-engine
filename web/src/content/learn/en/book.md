<h2 id="product-question">Question</h2>

Why does an order for 20 shares finish with only 15 filled?

<h2 id="product-try">Try it</h2>

Step through both fills. The first buys 5 shares at 101, and the second buys 10 more at 110. Unfilled shares fall from 20 to 15, then to 5. The fills share an economic clock and retain their event order.

<h2 id="product-read">Read the result</h2>

When the report arrives, reported shares become 15. Actual shares stay at 15 and the remaining 5 shares stay unfilled. The report acknowledges existing trades, with no further settlement.

Unfilled shares can miss a rise or avoid a fall. Measuring that outcome requires a benchmark and horizon. This case records no later outcome, so the missed opportunity remains unknown.

<h2 id="product-unknown">Evidence limits</h2>

Depth and report delay are synthetic illustrations. Real venue latency and queue position need separate measurements.
