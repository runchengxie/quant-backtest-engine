<h2 id="product-question">Question</h2>

If impact is already in the fill price, should cash pay it again?

<h2 id="product-try">Try it</h2>

Open once and step from Reserve cash to Settle once. Read Price and Shares first, then Gross payment, Fees and Cash. Finally inspect Impact inside price and the recorded fill table below.

<h2 id="product-read">Read the recorded result</h2>

The recorded settlement pays the gross amount and one cash fee. The impact figure explains part of the gross payment; subtracting it again would charge the same effect twice. The static fill table lets you trace the displayed amount back to its owner output.

<h2 id="product-unknown">What remains unknown</h2>

A descriptive missed-opportunity measure also is not a cash fee. This case proves its own reconciliation; it does not establish real spread, commission or impact calibration.
